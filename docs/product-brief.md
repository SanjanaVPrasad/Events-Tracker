# Product brief: Events Tracker

Status: personal prototype and public source portfolio copy. Market demand and outcome claims remain hypotheses.

## Audience and problem hypothesis

Working professionals exploring technology events in India may struggle to compare relevant opportunities and understand the full cost of attending. The initial scope focuses on discovery, shortlisting and budget planning rather than ticket transactions.

## Intended value

Help someone find a suitable event and estimate whether attending fits a learning budget. Proposed outcome measures are time to find a relevant event, successful shortlist creation, cost-estimation accuracy and repeat use. No measured savings, revenue or adoption is claimed.

## Current scope and constraints

The single-file HTML/CSS/JavaScript app includes city/type filters, static top picks, price sorting, My Plans, wishlist, hidden listings, ticket/cart estimates and custom costs. Event data is a manually maintained 2 November 2026 snapshot. Plans normally stay in browser localStorage. There is no backup interface, automatic discovery, ticket checkout or portable account sync.

## Product role and AI role

Sanjana supplied the personal event-discovery and budget-planning needs and used AI assistance in building the prototype. Portfolio evidence should show the actual requirements, decisions, review and checks she can explain. The current implementation is not a personalised AI recommender. Imported relevance labels do not establish model quality.

## Next decision

First establish trustworthy date classification, data freshness and recovery. Then observe five potential users finding, shortlisting and estimating an event. Expand only after understanding the repeated obstacle. See the [roadmap](roadmap.md).
